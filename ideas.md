# Petal & Crumb Bakery — Visual Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference Spec

The supplied reference is the definitive visual brief: a **high-key editorial bakery portfolio** with deliberate white space, restrained blush blocks, fine dark typography, large cake photography, thin dividers, and a composed modular rhythm. It is not an e-commerce template. Specific visual decisions in the supplied image take precedence over the broader copy brief.

## Chosen Approach: Quiet Patisserie Editorial

### Design Movement

Contemporary **editorial still-life** styling with the polished restraint of an independent pâtisserie lookbook. The visual language borrows the reference image’s art-directed product photography, uneven mosaic composition, and soft paper-like palette.

### Core Principles

1. **Photography leads, interface supports.** Large, spacious cake imagery creates desire; controls use fine rules and quiet labels.
2. **Asymmetry feels intentional.** Content is organized as editorial blocks and staggered columns rather than repeated card grids.
3. **Luxury through restraint.** Borders, shadows, color, and motion stay delicate, allowing negative space and typography to do the work.
4. **The order tool earns attention.** The quote workspace is clearer and more structured than the marketing sections, expressing the business value without breaking the brand tone.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is warm porcelain rather than stark white, echoing fondant and linen. A pale rose wash provides moments of softness; **rosewood terracotta** carries interactive emphasis and feels baked, tactile, and ownable. Ink is a softened brown-black so editorial headlines remain elegant rather than severe.

### Layout Paradigm

The homepage follows a vertical pastry counter: a generous header and photographic hero, an offset “note from the kitchen” feature, then a collage of editorial tiles and a long gallery strip. The custom-order route becomes a two-column studio table where the form occupies the work surface and the live total stays visible at its edge.

### Signature Elements

1. A **blush recipe-note block** with an oversized serif line break.
2. **Hairline framed product labels** that hover beneath image tiles.
3. A subtle **petal-and-crumb mark**: a four-petal flower formed from frosting swirls with a small crumb at its center.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like moving objects across a baker’s worktable: decisive but soft. Product imagery gently lifts on hover, selected form choices gain a thin rosewood outline, and the quote update is immediate and calm. Form steps never feel gamified.

### Animation

Use short opacity and translate transitions only: 180–240 ms for controls, 240 ms for section entrances, and a 60 ms stagger for editorial tile groups. The small signature delight is a single soft “bloom” pulse around the flower mark when the quote confirmation appears. All nonessential motion honors reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** is the expressive display face: high-contrast, slightly romantic, and set with tight tracking for major headings. **DM Sans** is the humanist utility face: clear at small sizes and used for labels, forms, and navigation. Headings are mostly sentence case; tiny interface labels use restrained uppercase tracking.

### Brand Essence

**Petal & Crumb is a custom cake studio for people who want their celebration to feel as considered as the dessert at its center.**

Personality: **considered, warm, artful**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are sensory and specific; CTA language is courteous, direct, and never pushy. Microcopy clarifies decisions without jargon.

> “A cake worth gathering around.”

> “Build your celebration, line by line.”

### Wordmark & Logo

Use an editorial wordmark with a custom ampersand feeling, paired with the standalone petal-and-crumb swirl symbol. The favicon and header use the symbol at a legible scale; the symbol does not depend on tiny text.

### Signature Brand Color

**Rosewood Glaze — `#A8515A`**. This deep, dusted pink is reserved for moments that need confidence: primary CTAs, active fields, and key price states.

## Style Decisions

- Stay high-key, photo-forward, and light enough that charcoal typography remains consistently readable.
- Avoid generic rounded-card interfaces; use modest radii only where a control needs softness.
- The requested customer testimonial section is intentionally omitted because fabricated reviews or quote placeholders are not appropriate for the demo. Trust is demonstrated through the transparent ordering workflow instead.

## Verification Notes

- The desktop order studio uses a 801px work surface and a 360px sticky quote panel, keeping the live estimate visible alongside the form.
- The availability calendar uses seven equal day columns and mock capacity states for open, lead-time, and fully booked days.
- The tested confirmation flow presents the selected date, fulfillment, itemized pricing, a 50% deposit, the remaining balance, and a mock automated balance-reminder handoff.
