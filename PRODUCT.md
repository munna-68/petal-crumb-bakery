# Petal & Crumb Bakery — Product Definition

## Users
Primary: Celebrating hosts (25–45) in Portland who treat dessert as the emotional centerpiece of birthdays, showers, and intimate weddings. They value design, seasonality, and personal service over convenience. Secondary: Curious browsers seeking inspiration before committing to a custom order; they need transparent pricing and date confidence quickly.

## Product Purpose
Petal & Crumb is a custom cake studio for people who want their celebration to feel as considered as the dessert at its center. The site is an editorial storefront plus a live order studio: a photo-forward brand experience that converts inspiration into a clear, itemized quote with real availability, no back-and-forth.

register: brand

## Brand / Tone
Considered, warm, artful. Sensory and specific in headlines, courteous and direct in CTAs, never pushy or gamified. The voice says "A cake worth gathering around" and "Build your celebration, line by line." Trust is shown through transparent workflow, not fabricated testimonials.

## Anti-References
Avoid: generic rounded-card e-commerce templates; bubbly cupcake-shop kitsch; SaaS pricing-table clichés; heavy drop shadows and plastic gloss; stock-photo perfection without texture.

## Strategic Principles
1. Photography leads, interface supports. Large airy cake imagery does the selling; controls stay delicate with fine rules and quiet labels.
2. Asymmetry feels intentional. Editorial blocks and staggered columns, not repeated identical grids.
3. Luxury through restraint. Negative space, typography, and hairline details carry the experience; color and motion remain soft.
4. The order tool earns attention. The quote workspace is clearer and more structured than marketing sections, making value legible without breaking tone.
